# GXEON Media Center Safety Policy

GXEON Media Center is designed for controlled AI media operations. AI may assist creation, organization, summarization, drafting, and analysis, but humans approve publishing decisions.

## Prohibited uses

- No spam automation.
- No mass DM automation.
- No impersonation.
- No voice cloning without authorization.
- No face cloning without authorization.
- No unauthorized use of third-party videos.
- No attempts to bypass platform controls, authentication, terms, or rate limits.

## Required controls

- Human approval is required before publishing content externally.
- Published content should include evidence logging, including source references, approval records, timestamps, and responsible operator where appropriate.
- Operators must respect official platform APIs, platform policies, and rate limits.
- Sensitive workspace data must remain behind authentication.
- External integrations must be reviewed before enablement and must not require committed secrets.
